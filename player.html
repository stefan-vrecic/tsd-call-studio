          Pre-Call Checklist

          • Lead with love and compassion
          • I lead the call, I do not give the power away
          • I cut through noise, bullshit and smoke screens
          • I have faith in my prospect
          • Why is my prospect so committed to following through despite uncertainty?
          • Why is my prospect so financially resourceful?
          • Money is Neutral (I have it in my hands and detached from which direction it goes)
          • They need me (I’m their only hope and that’s MY responsibility)
          • Breathe
          • I am the Master (presence and pro-athlete mentality)

          Review Checklist

          • Did the closer address all the fear, doubt?
          • Was the prospect empowered and trusting?
          • Which specific moments were weak?
          • Which language patterns indicate this?
          • Did the prospect show signs of financial resourcefulness?
          • Did the prospect show full signs of commitment?

          NLP Checklist & Statements

          • "While I may not automatically love this, how can I find a way to use it?"
          • "What one or two things can I do NOW to give this a chance to help myself?"
          • One of the worst things we can say in life is "that's not for me"
